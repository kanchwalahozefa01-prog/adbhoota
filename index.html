About Adbhoota

A cornucopia of ideas, shaped into exquisite form

Adbhoota is the creative studio of Hozefa Kanchwala and Shyam Sundar Chatterjee — always incubating joyful ideas and bringing our clients' imagination to life.

With emotional awareness and creative understanding, we shape a custom design process around every client, and deliver solutions that are minimal yet magical.

The people

Meet the minds behind the magic

Shyam Sundar Chatterjee

Animation Film Designer

Shyam is an artist at heart, a designer by process and an animator by expression. His practice combines observation, imagination and a thoughtful design process to transform ideas into compelling visual narratives. With experience working with clients across the world, he specialises in animation films, motion graphics, visual storytelling and mixed-media communication. His work has received national and international recognition.

🏆 National Film Award 2017 · Hum Chitra Banate Hai

Hozefa Kanchwala

Product & Design Consultant

Hozefa helps companies build AI-powered digital products and strengthen the design practices behind them. With over 12 years of experience as a designer and entrepreneur, he has worked with organisations including Cognizant, Royal London, Drip Capital and HUB Technology Partners. His expertise spans product strategy, design systems, and building and mentoring in-house design teams, with a focus on creating meaningful design outcomes that support business goals.
